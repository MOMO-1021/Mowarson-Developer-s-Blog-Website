Common positioning and messaging mistakes

  • Trying to appeal to everyone. {{Explain the cost of a position so broad it cannot guide a decision.}}
  • Confusing a feature with customer value. {{Show how to connect what the product does to what changes for the customer.}}
  • Claiming uniqueness without evidence. {{Explain how proof and experience support credibility.}}
  • Writing the tagline before the strategy. {{Explain why memorable words cannot compensate for unclear choices.}}
  • Changing the story in every channel. {{Show how inconsistent claims create doubt and how a message hierarchy helps.}}
